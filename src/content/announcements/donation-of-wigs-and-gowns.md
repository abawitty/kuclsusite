---
title: Donation of Wigs and Gowns
date: 2026-07-17
---
Students previously had to rely on wigs and gowns borrowed from the SRC for moot competitions — not always convenient or sustainable. Following engagement with stakeholders, the LSU received a generous donation of 9 wigs and gowns for use in upcoming moot competitions, easing a long-standing logistical barrier for the Moot Court Society.
