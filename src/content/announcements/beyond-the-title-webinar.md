---
title: "\"Beyond the Title\" Webinar"
date: 2026-08-06
---
To climax the semester's activities, the LSU partnered with the Women's Commissioner, Mrs. Christabel Faith Bampoe, to organise the "Beyond the Title" Webinar, empowering female law students with practical guidance on legal careers. The session featured Bernice Osei-Drah Acklerh, Nana Obu Afful, and Odamea Amoako, who shared insights on legal education, professional development, and career opportunities.

The recording has been uploaded to the George-More Legal Hub for students who were unable to attend.
