---
title: Launch of the George-More Legal Hub
date: 2026-06-19
---
The administration established the George-More Legal Hub, an online academic repository created to make past questions, recorded tutorial sessions, seminars, and other learning materials more accessible to law students — supplementing the LSU website. A QR code linking to the Hub has been displayed at strategic locations and notice boards around campus.

See the [Legal Hub section](/#) on the homepage, or scan the QR code on campus.
