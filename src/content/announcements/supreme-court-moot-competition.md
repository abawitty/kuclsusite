---
title: Supreme Court Moot Competition
date: 2026-07-20
---
The LSU took part in a national moot competition marking the 150th anniversary of the Supreme Court. Through the Moot Court Society, and with coaching support from Valentine Ogundare, Benjamin Addo, Clement Osei, and Mavis Bawa, Kings University College was represented by:

- Priscilla More
- Bright Obeng
- Constancia Blewushie
- Millicent Odonkor

The team performed satisfactorily and gained valuable exposure competing alongside students from other law faculties — experience the administration intends to apply to the upcoming Inter-Chamber Moot Competition.
